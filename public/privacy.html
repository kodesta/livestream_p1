Privacy Policy

Data Protection Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Livestream ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, password (stored securely as a hashed value, never in plain text), and optionally a display name.

Content You Upload

If you upload videos, images, or related metadata (titles, descriptions, actors, etc.), we store this content and associated information to display it on the Platform.

Usage Data

We collect information about how you interact with the Platform, including:

  • Reactions you leave on content
  • Titles you add to your Favorites
  • Search queries
  • Pages visited and general usage patterns

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies to keep you logged in (session cookies) and, where advertising is enabled, to support ad delivery through third-party networks such as Google AdSense. See Section 6 below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Allow you to upload, favorite, and react to content
  • Display personalized elements such as your Favorites list
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Platform
  • Respond to support requests and copyright/takedown notices
  • Display advertising (where applicable) and measure its performance

3. How We Store Your Information

Your account and content data is stored in a PostgreSQL database. Uploaded video and image files are stored using third-party cloud storage infrastructure. We take reasonable technical measures (such as password hashing) to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Platform (e.g., hosting providers, cloud storage providers, database providers).
  • Advertising partners (such as Google AdSense), where enabled, subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Legal authorities, if required by law, or to respond to valid legal process, protect our rights, or investigate fraud or security issues.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@livestream.com.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are located in South Africa, you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

6. Advertising and Cookies

Where advertising is enabled on the Platform, we may use Google AdSense or similar advertising networks. These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.
  • Where required by law (such as for users in the EEA/UK), we will request your consent before showing personalized advertising, using a consent management tool.
  • You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Livestream is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

8. International Users

If you access the Platform from outside the country in which our servers or service providers are located, your information may be transferred to and processed in a different country. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Uploaded content that is later removed for policy violations, or content subject to a valid copyright takedown notice, will be deleted from our storage systems.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at contact@livestream.com.
